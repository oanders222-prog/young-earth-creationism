An age-old conflict

The Biblical worldview is unique in teaching that the world is not eternal, that it had a beginning, and that this beginning is measurable by human genealogies. The ancient philosophers such as Plato and Aristotle taught that the world had no beginning. In our day, materialist evolutionists teach that the past must be explained only by appeal to naturalistic causes now seen operating at current rates.

This site addresses the logical fallacies at the heart of uniformitarianism and the false presuppositions behind its adherents and popularizers. It provides resources to argue that the earth is created by God and that this creation occurred within human memory as described by Biblical genealogies.

The argument over the age of the earth is not a new one, nor is it merely a “scientific” one. It is an argument between those who believe God the Creator and those who are in unbelief.
